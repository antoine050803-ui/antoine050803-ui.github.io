Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

GTA6 Clips Poster is a personal desktop tool run by Antoine Mahamoud Nour (Bordeaux, France) to publish gameplay clips to his own TikTok accounts.

Data collected

When a TikTok account is connected, the app receives the account's open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic), and an access token that allows video uploads (video.upload, video.publish).

How data is used

This data is only used to show which account is connected and to upload videos chosen by the account owner. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

All data is stored locally on the owner's computer, in the app's local database. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account stays connected. Disconnecting the account in the app, or revoking access in TikTok (Settings > Security and permissions > Apps), deletes the stored token.
